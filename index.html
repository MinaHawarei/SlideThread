Design scroll-stopping carousels in seconds

SlideThread is a free, privacy-first studio for carousels, posts, and stories for LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook — in English and Arabic. Everything runs in your browser, and your designs never leave your device.

What you can do with SlideThread

  • Ready-made templates. Start from a gallery of carousel and post templates and make them yours.
  • A full design editor. Layers, typography, colors, and brand styles with precise control over every element.
  • English & Arabic. Native right-to-left support with Arabic fonts, so bilingual content just works.
  • Paste text to slides. Turn an outline or draft into a structured slide deck automatically.
  • Export anywhere. Crisp PNG images, multi-page PDFs, and project files you can back up and reimport.
  • Private by design. Local-first: projects autosave on your device. No account, no uploads.

Frequently asked questions

What is SlideThread?

A free browser-based studio for designing social media carousels, posts, and stories — from a quick single post to a full multi-slide carousel.

Is SlideThread free?

Yes. You can design and export without paying anything.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the studio and start designing; your projects are saved automatically on your own device.

Where is my content stored?

On your device. SlideThread is local-first, so your text, images, and designs stay in your browser unless you choose to export them.

Which platforms and formats does it support?

Carousels, posts, and stories sized for LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook, in square, portrait, story, and wide aspect ratios. Export as PNG images or a multi-page PDF.

Does SlideThread support Arabic?

Yes. The studio works fully in English and Arabic, with right-to-left text and Arabic fonts built in.
