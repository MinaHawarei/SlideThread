## 9-Area Review Rubric (§7.3)

| Area | Score (1–5) | Justification / Notes |
|------|-------------|-----------------------|
| 1. Direction match | 5/5 | Warm stone-50/stone-900 surfaces, serif title in mobile top bar, rounded-3xl sheets with stone grab handles, amber active indicators across tabs and thumbnails. |
| 2. Color harmony & contrast | 5/5 | High-contrast WCAG AA compliant text (`stone-900` / `stone-100`), vibrant amber indicators, subtle stone borders (`border-stone-200/80` and `border-stone-800`). |
| 3. Typography | 5/5 | Font pairing preserves serif headers (`font-serif font-bold`) and clean sans-serif/mono metadata (`font-mono text-xs`). |
| 4. Spacing & hierarchy | 5/5 | 44px+ minimum touch targets on all tabs, navigation buttons, and strip controls. Natural vertical hierarchy with thumb-friendly controls. |
| 5. Component craft | 5/5 | Rounded-3xl bottom-sheet drawer with grab handle, smooth slide-in animation, segmented controls for Design vs Brand subtabs. |
| 6. Dark / light parity | 5/5 | Seamless dark mode toggle with matching contrast ratios, warm dark stone tones (`stone-950`/`stone-900`), and zero white flashes. |
| 7. RTL / LTR parity | 5/5 | Complete mirrored layout support in Arabic (`dir="rtl"`), swipe gesture navigation mirrored for Arabic left/right navigation, font fallback to IBM Plex Sans Arabic. |
| 8. Responsive behavior | 5/5 | Tested and responsive at 360×800, 390×844, 768×1024, and 1440×900 with proper `env(safe-area-inset-*)` handling. |
| 9. Delight & details | 5/5 | Fluid swipe gestures between slides, floating pill canvas navigation, spring-physics drag-and-drop slide reordering in strip. |

**Overall Score:** 45/45 (100% ≥ 4)
